FIRST LIGHT

Three things to try.

  1. Choose a fixture and a layer. Add a Constant, then a dimming or color block. Changes appear in the live scene.
  2. Give it some motion. Add a property driver, a spatial field or a visual Bézier curve. Save the result in Projects.
  3. Connect your output. In Output, define a model, check its channel footprint, then enable Art-Net for your receiver.

Art-Net starts disabled. Custom DMX models describe every slot, including fixed-value program channels. The phone controls the same hosted scene.
